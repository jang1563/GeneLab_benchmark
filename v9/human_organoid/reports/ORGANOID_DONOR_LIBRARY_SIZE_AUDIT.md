- Donor-level medians vary substantially and are coupled to source/organoid
  fate/disease context. This supports keeping donor-aware analyses diagnostic.

## Decision

Keep the default baseline as:

```text
transform=log1p
scaling=zscore
top_variable_genes=2000
```

Do not promote `tvg2000_none_zscore` despite its higher balanced accuracy and
AUROC. The raw-count sensitivity result remains unresolved because sample-total,
sparsity, donor, source, and disease-context diagnostics show label-associated
or design-associated scale differences.

## Remaining Blocker

The next evidence needed is not whether donor/iPSC-line metadata exists; it
does. The blocker is split design: the current public sample set is too small
and too coupled to treat donor holdout as a clean donor-generalization benchmark.
The next task should decide whether donor-aware folds stay diagnostic-only or
whether a separate task family should be created with very conservative claim
language.

## Production Interpretation

B3/B4 are now technically ready for careful prototype rendering.

They are not yet visually approved. The next renderer must still satisfy the
post-render gate:

1. render `scene_plate.png`, `rendered_preview.png`, and
   `rendered_preview.pdf`;
2. inspect the PNG at full size;
3. inspect thumbnail/contact-sheet behavior;
4. verify no text overlap or clipping;
5. verify status/caveat visibility;
6. verify that the slide does not look like a dashboard, warning sign, or
   software architecture diagram;
7. update `qa.json` to `post_render` only after visual inspection.

## Next Technical Move

Create `scripts/build_bridge_method_scenes.py` only after the renderer brief
chooses the exact B3/B4 geometry.

The renderer should consume the existing contract files rather than hard-code
headline/caveat/evidence metadata again. That keeps slide wording and QA in one
place and makes iteration safer.

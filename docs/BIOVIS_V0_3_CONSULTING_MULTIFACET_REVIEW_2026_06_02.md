This is the key improvement over decorative biological motifs.

## Consulting-Grade Rule Set

For future slides, each biological visual should answer:

- What executive or scientific question does it answer?
- Is it context, construct, observation, or validated result?
- What species, modality, and biological level does it represent?
- What claim must the caption prevent?
- What decision follows from the visual?

If a visual cannot answer these, it should not be in the deck.

## Recommendation

Keep v0.3 as the current controlled-use biological visual system.

Use immediately for:

- bridge slides;
- method explanation;
- scope maps;
- internal decision roadmaps;
- consulting-style narrative scaffolds.

Do not rely on v0.3 alone for:

- final manuscript proof panels;
- high-profile result slides;
- claims requiring real microscopy, histology, spatial, or omics evidence.
